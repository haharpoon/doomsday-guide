Free-space aerial lengths are the speed-of-light sum: wavelength in metres is 300 divided by the frequency in MHz. They are not a quote from a licence document.

Not nailed down, so the cards stay soft on purpose:

- A single worldwide "recall" whistle. The cards use a group code of one blast and two blasts, and say to agree it first.
- The exact day BBC Radio 4 long wave ended. The BBC's current FM and AM list simply does not include it.
- Whether every PMR446 brand uses the same 38-tone list. The card says to test the pair in one room.
- Box-top range in kilometres. Ofcom calls PMR446 short range. The card tells you to measure your own pair.
- The amateur exam's current question count. Check the RSGB. It has changed before.
