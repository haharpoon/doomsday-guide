## Square lashing

Use: join two poles that cross at a right angle. Shelter frames, racks, ladders.

1. Clove hitch on the upright pole, just below where the crossbar sits. Twist the tail round the cord.

2. Wrap up over the crossbar, behind the upright, down over the crossbar on the other side, behind the upright. Like a square.

3. Do 3 or 4 full wraps. Keep each one tight and beside the last, not on top.

4. Frapping: 2 or 3 turns between the poles, around the wraps. Pull hard. This tightens everything.

5. Finish with a clove hitch on the crossbar.

## Diagonal lashing

Use: poles that cross at an angle, or that spring apart. Cross-braces.

1. Timber hitch around both poles, across the diagonal. Pull the poles together.

2. 3 or 4 wraps along that same diagonal.

3. 3 or 4 wraps across the other diagonal.

4. 2 or 3 frapping turns between the poles, around the wraps.

5. Finish with a clove hitch on one pole.

![diagonal lashing: wraps both diagonals then frap](img/lash_diagonal.png)

## Tripod lashing

Use: a tripod for a cooking pot, water filter, or shelter frame.

1. Lay three poles side by side. Put the middle pole the opposite way round.

2. Clove hitch on one outer pole.

3. Weave the cord over and under the poles, 5 or 6 loose turns. Too tight and the legs will not spread.

4. Frap 2 turns between each pair of poles.

5. Clove hitch on the other outer pole.

6. Stand it up. Spread the legs, crossing the middle pole over.
